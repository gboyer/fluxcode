fluxcode & fluxprune

This repository contains two complementary tools for scientific and engineering time series: fluxcode for storage compression and fluxprune for volume reduction. You can use either one on its own, or chain them together—pruning a high-volume series first and then packing the remaining (time, value) points with fluxcode.

fluxcode — reduce storage with minimal loss

Packs float64 values and optional exact int64 timestamps into self-describing block groups. Every sample is preserved within a strict error bound, exact binary, decimal, and rational/ADC grids decode bit-for-bit, and decoding runs at multi-GB/s on a single core.

fluxprune — reduce data volume itself

A pre-pass downsampler that drops redundant points and keeps the subset that carries a series' shape for linear interpolation. Fewer stored points means faster scans, plots, and downstream analytics.
