fluxcode

fluxcode compresses float64 time series for scientific and engineering telemetry. It bounds quantization error per block, detects binary, decimal, and rational/ADC grids automatically, and can store exact int64 timestamps alongside the values.

On a single Apple M3 core, it decodes at several GB/s and packs typical sensor series to 1–6 bits per sample (compared to 64 for raw float64). Use the navigation bar above or the cards below to browse the format specification and benchmark reports.
